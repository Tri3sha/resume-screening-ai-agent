JOB TITLE: Data Analyst

We are looking for a Data Analyst to join our analytics team.

Requirements:

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Science,
  Statistics, Mathematics, or a related field.
- Strong Python programming skills.
- Strong SQL knowledge.
- Experience with Pandas and NumPy.
- Experience with data visualization.
- Knowledge of Power BI or Tableau.
- Understanding of statistics and data analysis.
- Experience working with machine learning is a plus.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.

Preferred experience:

- 1–3 years of experience in data analytics.
- Experience working with large datasets.
- Experience creating dashboards and reports.
